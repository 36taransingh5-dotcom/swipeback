how it works

01

pay or snap

swipe the company card, or photograph the receipt and send it in slack. no app to install, no forms to fill.

02

ai checks it

merchant, amount, date and category come off the receipt, get matched to the card spend, and are checked against the company's policy and budget.

03

cleared or flagged

fair spend clears itself and the reimbursement is queued. anything dodgy lands with a finance reviewer, reasons on screen. finance only deals with exceptions.

where you use it

slack live

dm a receipt photo to the bot, get the outcome in a thread under your message.

email + card feed live

the card notification email starts the check; receipts and replies stay in one thread.

reviewer dashboard live

the exception queue and case page for finance. it exists only for what ai won't clear.

imessage coming soon

text a receipt photo like you would to a friend. on the roadmap.

why finance trusts it

audit trail

every decision, email and payout is hash-chained. tamper-evident by construction.

one-time approvals

approval links work once, for one person, on one version of an expense. they never sign anyone in.

humans decide the edge cases

ai clears the routine. people decide the dodgy, with the evidence and the reasons in front of them.

demo moves no money

payouts are simulated end to end. the plumbing is real, the money is not.
