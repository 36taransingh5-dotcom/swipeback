A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad
you asked.

Do employees need another app?+

No. Employees send a receipt to the finance inbox, or reply when asked for one. Questions and confirmations stay in the email thread.

Do we have to switch company cards?+

Swipeback is designed to work alongside your existing cards. Card-feed availability depends on your provider; the current preview uses sample card transactions.

What if a receipt is missing something?+

Swipeback asks a focused question in the same email thread. If it still needs a judgement call, finance gets the context to decide.

Can I try it here?+

Yes. Explore the three expense examples above to see a routine approval, a follow-up, and a finance review. This is a product preview with sample data; payments are simulated.
